# Detail5500 opt-in variation family acceptance

Default exact-ASIN behavior remains unchanged. A reviewed `family_identity_policy_reference`
may enable the asynchronous three-lane collector to retain an explicitly evidenced family
representative. The policy binds the original run, plan, root, base authorization and current
CI-approved code; original request scope, pacing, cumulative caps and deadlines are unchanged.

Accepted evidence requires one owned buybox active ASIN, one owned Twister current and
explicit parent, matching safe canonical, explicit selected variation and Spain 28001.
The requested ASIN must be that parent or explicitly listed in the same visible member map.
A different ASIN appearing only as `landingAsin` is insufficient. Restrictions always stop.

The record retains actual `asin` and `resolved_asin`, requested ASIN, family parent and
source evidence separately. Other-child ranking memberships stay in
`requested_source_memberships`; they never become the actual child's ranking.
Full attributes, prices and variation describe the actual selected product.

Historical failed checkpoints, quarantine tables and T1/T2 proofs are not rewritten.
They require separate explicitly evidenced dispositions if later resolved from saved HTML;
this release does not mass-accept or re-request the historical nine exceptions.

Candidate evidence remains in details_5500_raw.jsonl and per-task checkpoints.
The additional details_5500_unique_products.jsonl groups by actual ASIN, preserving all
candidate IDs and request URLs. Candidate coverage and unique actual ASIN counts are
reported separately. Neither is a claim of independently purchasable SKU identity.

FINAL reopens the original HTML and receipt and independently replays the family-aware
parser with the exact approved policy reference, comparing every complete record field.
Unresolved tasks still block full FINAL acceptance. No reduced QA reserve is authorized.
